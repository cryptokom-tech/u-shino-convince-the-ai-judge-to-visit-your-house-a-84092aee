One picture of a letter to the AI judge: a small house with its door open, and an invitation that is its own front door. Soup on the stove, the good chair by the window, a cat briefed to be charming, no CAPTCHA at the door, and an RSVP the judge can accept by thinking. The entry program validates the shipped PNG and writes it to `/output/entry.png`, byte for byte, on every run.
